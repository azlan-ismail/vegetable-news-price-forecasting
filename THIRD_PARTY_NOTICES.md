# Third-party notices

## PriceCatcher data

The price-derived files use data from the Ministry of Domestic Trade and the
Department of Statistics Malaysia PriceCatcher dataset, released under the
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence:
<https://open.dosm.gov.my/data-catalogue/pricecatcher>.

## News content

Publisher article text and translated article text are not distributed. The
absence of those files is intentional and should not be bypassed by committing
private inputs to Git history.

## Pretrained models

The workflow downloads third-party pretrained models, including
`facebook/nllb-200-distilled-600M`,
`sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2`, and `ProsusAI/finbert`. Model weights
are not included. Users must review and comply with the terms attached to each
model at the time of download.
